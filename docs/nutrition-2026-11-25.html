Clinical nutrition ESPEN · Body mass index and the risk of first hip fracture in older Chinese adults: a longitudinal analysis of the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study with death as a competing event

每降低 1 kg/m² BMI，髖部骨折風險顯著增加（HR 0.95）。低 BMI (<18.5) 群體之累積髖部骨折風險最高（4.6%），支撐「維持健康 BMI」作為長者跌倒與骨折預防策略。
